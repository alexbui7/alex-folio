Hi, I'm **Alex (Bui Minh Anh)** — an infrastructure engineer who builds automation-first cloud platforms for regulated, large-scale environments.

At **Vietcombank**, I design and build the foundations of the bank's enterprise data and ML platform on **AWS and Databricks**: multi-account architecture, secure private networking, identity and access, Infrastructure-as-Code, CI/CD, and the governance and FinOps controls that keep it all accountable.

What I enjoy most is the part between people and systems — turning ambiguous requirements into measurable engineering criteria, and getting engineering teams, security, vendors, and leadership aligned on a technical strategy they can actually ship.

**What I work on**

- **Cloud platform architecture** — AWS multi-account design, networking, and platform governance
- **Data & ML platforms** — Databricks, Unity Catalog, MLflow, model serving
- **Automation** — Terraform, Databricks Asset Bundles, GitLab CI/CD
- **Security & identity** — Entra ID, AWS IAM, SSO/SCIM, RBAC
- **Cost & operations** — FinOps, tagging and cost allocation, observability
